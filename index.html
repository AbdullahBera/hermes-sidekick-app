Hermes

Hermes is a personal assistant that one person runs on their own computer, for their own use. It is not a public service: there are no accounts to sign up for and no other users.

With the owner's permission it can read their Google Calendar and Gmail, so that it can answer questions like "what's on today?" or "anything important in my inbox?". It cannot send, change or delete email or calendar events.

The data it reads is processed by an AI model running on the owner's own computer and is not shared with anyone.
